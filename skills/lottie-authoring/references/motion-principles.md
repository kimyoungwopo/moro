# Motion principles: communicate a state change

These are craft suggestions to test visually, not measured conversion or quality claims.

| Intent | Current-model technique | Failure to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| Guide attention | Offset one x/y move before a restrained scale change | Several layers peak at once |
| Imply weight | Short lift, faster travel, small settle, then hold | Repeated bounce distracts from destination |
| Confirm completion | Reveal overlapping check bars on a stable plate | Second bar reads before the first joins |
| Show waiting | Repeating stagger with no new progress meaning | Loader looks like a completion counter |
| Show interaction | Subject travels to a receiving surface; feedback follows contact | Feedback fires before arrival |

Uniform `scale` cannot squash one axis independently. Use a small overshoot (e.g. 106→100), not a claim of physically accurate deformation. There is no parenting: keep coordinated geometry aligned explicitly. Do not add follow-through to every layer; preserve a quiet reference surface.

Completion: pause at anticipation, contact, settle and hold. The last useful pose should communicate the intended state without relying on playback. Reduced-motion delivery should use that pose or a stable waiting still rather than simply running the loop more slowly.
